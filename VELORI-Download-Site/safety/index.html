Your privacy and device security matter. Here are practical things to check.

01

Verify your download

Use the domain you control and check the checksum on Downloads & verification. A matching checksum establishes the file matches this published copy; it does not prove the APK is harmless.

02

Respect Android security prompts

If the system flags malware or other risks, stop the installation. Do not disable Play Protect or other device safeguards simply to complete an APK install.

03

Protect your login

Use a unique password, never give out one-time codes, and verify messages claiming to be from platform support.

04

Control what you share

Think carefully before posting personal information, live locations, private images, or information about other people.

05

Respond to abusive behavior

Block or report unwanted conduct through features available on the VELORI platform. If you are in immediate danger, seek help from local emergency services or a trusted adult.

06

Check platform permissions

Before granting camera, photos, microphone or notification access, check why the app needs those permissions. Change them in Android settings when appropriate.
